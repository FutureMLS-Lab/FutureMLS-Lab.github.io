01 / Mechanism01 / 方法

Move the scale and offset.将缩放和偏移折入注意力计算

Packing KV reduces memory traffic. Reconstructing each value as s · code + z can still bottleneck decode. FOSCAR builds Tensor Core operands from packed codes and moves the affine work to group-level inputs and partial results.

Compare the two folds for token-wise quantization.比较逐 token 量化中的两种折叠方式。

Scale the QK partial result

For one token-wise K group, its scale and offset are shared along the head dimension.

  1. 1 · DotQ × K codes
  2. 2 · FoldScale and add offset term
  3. 3 · ContinueSoftmax on corrected logits
logit = sK · dot(Q, K codes) + zK · sum(Q)

Illustration: Q = [1, 2, 1, 1], codes = [0, 1, 2, 3], s = 0.5, z = −1. Reconstruct first: Q · [−1, −0.5, 0, 0.5] = −1.5. On B200, FP4 operands give dot(Q, y) = 3.5. Fold: 1 × 3.5 − 1 × 5 = −1.5.

Scale attention weights before PV

For token-wise V, scales vary along the token axis that PV reduces over.

  1. 1 · FoldMultiply P by each V scale
  2. 2 · DotUse scaled P with V codes
  3. 3 · CorrectAdd the weighted offsets
output = Σj (Pj · sj) · V codesj + Σj Pj · zj

Illustration: P = [0.25, 0.75], codes = [1, 3], s = [0.5, 1], z = [−1, −1]. On B200, fold s′ = 2s into P, giving [0.25, 1.5]. The FP4 dot product is 2.375. Add the weighted offset −1 to get 1.375.

The algebra is equivalent to dequantizing the same cache in exact arithmetic. For channel-wise grouping, K instead folds into Q and V scales the group-local PV accumulator. Paper §4.1 and Figures 3–5 ↗
